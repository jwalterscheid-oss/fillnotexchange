How to read the collar stamp

Look at the metal collar or handle around the valve. Dates are stamped into the metal, not a paper sticker. This is how to read a stamp — not how to fill a tank. We do not publish DIY fill instructions.

  • Original manufacture (12-year stamp): usually month then year, like 08 14 for August 2014. For a typical portable grill cylinder, that first stamp is good for 12 years. After that a licensed filler should refuse until a DOT requalifier puts a new date on the collar.
  • An E recert: after requalification you often see a newer month-year plus the letter E. E means an external visual inspection by an approved requalifier. That interval is typically 5 years, not another 12. A different letter (often S) marks a different test method with a longer interval.
  • A fill-station “visual check” before they pump is not a DOT recert. You need a requalifier stamp or laser etch on the collar.

PHMSA RIN locator — a check, not this directory

The official DOT list of approved cylinder requalifiers is the PHMSA RIN locator. Search by city or ZIP. It can be stale — shops drop recert without the locator catching up.
